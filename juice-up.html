About Juice Up.

Our first slot. A sunny beach bar run by a grinning glass of orange juice in sunglasses, open from the afternoon until well after dark.

Juice Up is built on Stackways: stacked symbols expand to fill their reels, so one spin can open up far more ways to win than it started with.

Like every Tumble game, it's designed, animated and mathed in-house. RTP, volatility and max win are published at launch.

How Stackways works.

Three steps on every spin. Full rules are published with the game.

  1. Spin

    Symbols land across the reels as normal.

  2. Stack

    Stack symbols expand, filling their reel with more symbols.

  3. Win more ways

    Every stack adds symbols to its reel, so the number of ways to win climbs with it.
