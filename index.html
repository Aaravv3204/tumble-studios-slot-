Who we are

Tumble Studios is a slot game studio founded in 2026. We're a small team of artists, mathematicians and engineers who think a slot should feel like a cartoon you can play: a world with a mood, a character with a face, and reels that never sit still.

We build everything in-house, from the first sketch to the certified math model. That keeps the games consistent and means there's one team to call when a partner needs something changed.

How we build

Four things every Tumble game has before it ships.

  • Cascading reels

    Winning symbols clear and new ones tumble in. One spin can keep paying, and every game has its own twist on the fall.

  • A character in every game

    Each title is led by a mascot with a personality, drawn and animated by our own artists. Players remember faces, not paytables.

  • In-house math

    Volatility, hit rate and RTP are designed alongside the art, not bolted on. Models are built for certification from day one.

  • Built for every screen

    HTML5 games that load fast and play the same on a phone in portrait as on a desktop, with no plugin and no app.
